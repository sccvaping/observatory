External review & scrutiny

Designed to be inspected before conclusions are published.

SCC Vaping separates public publication from controlled external scrutiny. Reviewers can inspect selected reports, release candidates and supporting material without receiving unrestricted access to the private research backend.

01

Internal research

Collection, processing and working analysis remain in the private research environment.

02

Review pack

Selected files, reports, methods and release candidates are assembled for scrutiny.

03

External scrutiny

Invited reviewers use the restricted SCC Vaping VVIP portal to inspect approved material.

04

Publication gate

Only approved, publication-safe outputs enter the public Observatory.

Restricted reviewer portal: access is managed separately from the public site. Review material should be shared with named authorised Google accounts rather than by exposing raw Drive folders publicly.
