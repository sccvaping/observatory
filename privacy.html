Privacy

Public evidence, minimal tracking.

The SCC Vaping public Observatory is designed to publish research infrastructure and public evidence outputs without requiring visitors to create an account.

Analytics

Analytics should only be enabled after the SCC Vaping analytics property, consent configuration and privacy documentation have been verified. This initial migration release does not embed a Google Analytics tracking script.

Cookies

The initial public site does not intentionally set non-essential first-party cookies. Third-party destinations linked from this site, including GitHub, SCC Nexus and Google Sites, operate under their own privacy terms.

Research data

The public repository is restricted to publication-safe aggregate and audit metadata. Private research records, working files, news leads and restricted review materials belong in the private research and controlled review environments rather than this public site.

Contact and governance

Project contact details and the formal controller/privacy wording should be added here once the SCC Nexus portfolio-wide privacy notice and contact route are finalised. No legal or organisational status is implied beyond the public project description.
